Senior Seminar — Capstone Project

“A comparison of pH, nitrate and phosphate levels found in eutrophied and non-eutrophied ocean zones.”

Summary

Compared pH, nitrate, and phosphate between eutrophied and non‑eutrophied North American coastal sites using NOAA CODAP‑NA data. Findings indicated higher nitrate/phosphate in eutrophied zones and regionally lower pH—suggesting eutrophication may intensify local acidification. Methods included site‑pair selection, depth control (0–200 m), and ANOVA in Excel.
