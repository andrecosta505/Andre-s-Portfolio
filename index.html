Featured Project — Senior Seminar Capstone

“A comparison of pH, nitrate and phosphate levels found in eutrophied and non-eutrophied ocean zones.”

Summary

Compared pH, nitrate, and phosphate levels between eutrophied and non-eutrophied North American coastal sites using NOAA CODAP-NA data. Findings indicated higher nitrate and phosphate in eutrophied zones and regionally lower pH, suggesting eutrophication may intensify local acidification. Methods included site-pair selection, depth control from 0–200 m, data reduction, and ANOVA in Excel.
